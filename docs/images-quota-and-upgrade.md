# Images quota visibility and upgrade readiness

Status: Phase 1 product specification, pending final plan entitlements and merchant approval. This is not a payment enablement decision or a declaration that Images is deployed.

## Scope and source of truth

Images at `im.ages.tw` will launch with image/GIF upload, optimization, and sharing, without video processing. An authenticated owner needs to understand both storage consumption and any enforced image-count limit before starting another upload.

Reuse the existing Library storage card and the owner-only `GET /api/me` quota response rather than building a second quota ledger. Today the response exposes `storage_limit_bytes`, `storage_used_bytes`, and `storage_reserved_bytes`, and the Library already renders a storage `<progress>` element. A per-image count/limit and Images-specific entitlement are **not yet implemented**. D1 remains authoritative; KV, browser state, and Cloudflare's account-level R2 allowance are not entitlement sources.

## Quota panel contract

- Show **storage consumed / storage allowed**, a visible percentage when the allowance is positive, and **storage remaining**. For `storage_limit_bytes > 0`, calculate the admission-oriented percentage as `100 * (storage_used_bytes + storage_reserved_bytes) / storage_limit_bytes`, clamped to `0..100` for the progress element. With a zero allowance, **omit the percentage and progress meter**, explicitly show `0` remaining and "No storage capacity available," and block new uploads without dividing by zero. Show persisted bytes and pending reservations separately so the text does not misleadingly describe pending uploads as completed storage. Remaining admission capacity is `max(0, limit - used - reserved)`.
- If an **image-count limit is enforced and positive**, render a second, separately labelled count meter: counted images / allowed images, percentage, and remaining image slots. If the enforced count cap is zero, report "No image slots available," with zero remaining and no percentage/meter. If no count cap is enforced, display the count as informational text **without** a misleading percentage or invented limit. Both limits are independent; reaching either one must prevent an upload without altering existing assets.
- Keep accounting consistent with the actual quota policy. Storage in Trash remains charged until successful physical purge; pending and still-reserved failed upload intents consume capacity until released. If a count cap is introduced, specify and enforce its counted statuses atomically (including live reservations and retained Trash) before displaying that cap. An Asset reused across timelines or products must not be counted as multiple objects.
- The current app's `usage_quotas` table (`migrations/0001_identity.sql`) and owner-only `/api/me` are **account-wide**, not Images-specific. Until a separately approved Images-specific entitlement exists, label the reported total as account-wide and do not assume other future Ages products have distinct or shared allowances. Introduce a product-scoped counter only after its ownership and entitlement model is approved; avoid an additive counter that silently double-charges shared assets.
- Reconcile the panel with authoritative owner-scoped responses after upload intent/completion, failure/expiration, trash/restore, and confirmed purge, including reloads and concurrent tabs. Avoid stale responses decreasing a newer known total. On errors, show an explicit unavailable state rather than `0%`, negative remaining space, `NaN`, or a fabricated unlimited allowance.
- Provide explanatory text for `0%`, approaching the limit (suggested UI thresholds: 80% and 95%), and quota exhausted. A full meter must identify whether **bytes**, **image slots**, or both are exhausted. Do not auto-delete non-expiring authenticated memories to make room.
- All numeric information must be readable without color or hover; use semantic progress labels and live announcements only for meaningful changes, with desktop/mobile/keyboard and English/Traditional Chinese coverage.

## Upgrade entry while checkout is unavailable

- Place a discoverable **Upgrade plan** affordance beside the quota panel and near an exhausted-limit message. Until merchant review and end-to-end payment entitlement gates pass, visibly label it **Subscriptions not yet available** (Traditional Chinese: **訂閱功能尚未開放**). The safe action opens the canonical, bilingual, public information page at `https://ages.tw/pricing`, never a fake checkout, payment prompt, or unimplemented purchase API.
- The canonical pricing page may describe proposed Free/Plus/Creator/Pro plans, but must distinguish proposals from purchasable features. Do not present unpublished storage or file-count numbers as promised benefits, and do not imply that clicking the link grants more capacity.
- Keep plan definitions, effective storage and optional image-count limits, and future upgrade availability **server-driven** so pricing/entitlements can change without rewriting the quota UI. Do not derive effective allowances from a client-side plan label or unlock paid limits on redirect success. Enabling purchases requires approved merchant onboarding, durable checkout, verified provider events, authoritative entitlements, dispute/refund handling, and an explicit rollout decision; see [payment-foundation.md](payment-foundation.md).
- The CTA is an informational navigation during Phase 1, not a request to enable a gateway. Avoid collecting payment details, silently registering a waiting list, exposing private quota data on public routes, or enabling a provider SDK.

## Capacity policy and operational budget

Per-user numbers require separate product approval. **50 MiB per free user and 50 stored images**, and **8 GB of total Images storage** have been discussed as **planning examples only**; they are **not** current app defaults, finalized plan entitlements, guaranteed free storage, or automatically enforced Cloudflare limits. The existing private-beta account default is 1 GiB with a 25 MiB image upload ceiling; do not override unrelated Stages workflows as a side effect of Images planning.

Before public launch, choose explicit per-format file limits, storage and optional image-count entitlements, server-side atomic admission rules, authenticated-upload rate bounds, moderation and reporting, and a separately monitored global storage budget that includes originals, stored variants, incomplete uploads, and retained Trash. Cloudflare R2's free monthly usage allowance belongs to the account, not to an individual user or Worker environment. Alerts alone are not an upload hard-stop; an operational hard-stop needs a race-safe, server-authoritative decision. Do not enable payment processing to handle exhausted quotas.

## Acceptance and ownership

1. The Images panel displays accurate nonnegative bytes, remaining bytes, percent, and pending-reservation breakdown from the real owner quota. An optional image-count limit appears only after an authoritative API and atomic server enforcement exist; either cap's rejection is clearly explained.
2. At exact boundary, after simultaneous upload intents, after deleting to Trash, after delayed purge, and after stale refresh, the panel and admission decision agree. A foreign or logged-out user never receives another owner's usage.
3. At 0%, 80%, 95%, 100%, a zero allowance (explicitly exhausted with no percentage), a temporarily unavailable API, and an unset image-count limit, accessible bilingual mobile/desktop UI renders meaningful text and does not promise unavailable storage.
4. The informational upgrade entry opens `https://ages.tw/pricing` and plainly states that subscriptions are not yet available; no checkout, provider call, subscription activation, paid entitlement, or new billing configuration occurs.
5. Exact published plan limits and global operational budget are approved and verified separately before public uploads; the feature remains decoupled from the later paid-subscription implementation.
